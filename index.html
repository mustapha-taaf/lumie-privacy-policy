Lumie Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Terms of service

This Privacy Policy explains how Advertise With Lumie ("Lumie", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Lumie mobile application, website, and services (together, the "Services").

By registering for and using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. You give separate, explicit consent to location-data collection during registration and can review or withdraw it at any time in the app (Profile → Privacy & Consent).

1. Who we are

Lumie operates a network of rooftop LED advertising screens installed on vehicles. Drivers and vehicle owners register their vehicles to display advertising; advertisers create campaigns that are delivered to those screens. Lumie is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. Questions: contact us using the details in Section 10.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a salted hash), account role, and profile photo.
  • Identity & verification documents: Ghana Card number, driver's licence number, date of birth, address, and photos of your documents and yourself (used to verify your identity and eligibility).
  • Vehicle information: registration number, make, model, colour, type, operating region, daily routes, and vehicle photos/documents.
  • Advertiser business information: company name, registration number, industry, contact person, contact email/phone, and address.
  • Payment and payout details: bank name, account number/name, or mobile money number used for payouts, and payment transaction records.
  • Support correspondence: anything you send us when contacting support.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location and route data (via the LED screen device): when a rooftop screen is installed on your vehicle, the device reports GPS coordinates, speed, heading, and route history so Lumie can verify campaign displays, measure coverage, and operate geofences. This data is collected by the screen device, not by your phone. Your consent to this collection is requested separately during registration.
  • Device and usage data: app version, device platform, push-notification tokens, and basic diagnostics needed to keep the Services working.
  • Screen telemetry: online/offline status, health metrics (e.g. screen temperature, brightness, voltage), and playback/display logs tied to campaigns.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not require access to your phone's precise location, contacts, messages, or camera roll beyond the documents and photos you choose to upload. The mobile app does not track your phone's location.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account, verify your identity, and process registrations and approvals;
  • Register, approve, and manage vehicles and screen installations;
  • Deliver advertising campaigns to screens, verify displays occurred, and calculate earnings;
  • Process campaign payments (advertisers) and payouts (drivers/owners), including refunds, receipts, and withdrawal requests;
  • Send service, approval, earnings, and campaign notifications;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access, and enforce our terms;
  • Provide support and improve the Services;
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, anti-money-laundering, records).

4. Legal bases for processing

We rely on the following legal bases (under applicable data-protection law):

  • Performance of a contract — providing the Services you registered for (account, campaigns, payouts);
  • Consent — location-data collection and marketing communications;
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, analytics, and improving the Services, where your interests do not override ours;
  • Legal obligation — tax and financial record-keeping.

5. How we share information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • Advertisers: aggregated, non-identifying campaign performance (coverage, impressions, routes) — advertisers cannot see your identity or exact personal routes.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:
    • AWS (S3) — document and creative file storage;
    • Paystack — payment processing and payouts;
    • Twilio — SMS delivery of verification codes;
    • Google Firebase / FCM — push notifications;
    • DataDog — monitoring, logs, and crash reporting;
    • MQTT broker / cloud host — screen device communication and hosting.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above:

  • Account data: while your account is active.
  • Verification documents: for the duration of your participation and a reasonable period afterwards to satisfy record-keeping and audit needs.
  • Financial records: for the period required by applicable tax law (typically 5–7 years), after which financial records are anonymized.
  • GPS/location logs: raw points are automatically purged after a configurable retention window (90 days by default).
  • Deleted accounts: when you delete your account (or we delete it), your personal information is anonymized and the account can no longer be used or signed into.

7. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption and hashing at rest for passwords and secrets, role-based access control on all services, per-device authentication for screen devices, and continuous security monitoring. No system is 100% secure — please report suspected vulnerabilities to the contact below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal information;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your account and personal information (available in-app under Profile → Delete Account, subject to outstanding balances/withdrawals);
  • Withdraw consent to location-data collection (see Section 9);
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured format;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any right, contact us (Section 10). We respond within the time required by law.

9. Location data & consent

Location data is central to Lumie's business: it verifies that ads were actually displayed where expected. Specific points:

  • Location is collected by the LED screen device installed on your vehicle and reported to Lumie — not by your phone.
  • You are asked for separate, explicit consent to this collection during registration and can see your consent status any time (Profile → Privacy & Consent).
  • Location data is used for display verification, analytics, geofencing, and earnings calculation. It is not sold and is not shared with advertisers in a form that identifies you.
  • You can withdraw consent by contacting support; withdrawal may prevent your vehicle from participating in campaigns (since display verification depends on location).
